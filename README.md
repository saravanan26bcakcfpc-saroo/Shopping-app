# ShopEase – Shopping App UI/UX Design 

## Project Overview

ShopEase is a modern and user-friendly shopping application UI/UX design created using Figma. The application allows users to explore products, browse categories, view product details, add items to their shopping cart, and proceed through the checkout process.The design focuses on a clean interface, attractive product images, simple navigation, and a smooth online shopping experience.

##  Objectives
- To design an attractive and user-friendly shopping application.
- To make product discovery simple and convenient.
- To organize products into easy-to-browse categories.
- To display product images, prices, ratings, and descriptions.
- To simplify the shopping cart and checkout process.
- To provide a consistent and enjoyable user experience.
- To demonstrate the complete shopping journey through Figma prototyping.

## Design Highlights
- **Modern UI:** Clean layouts with attractive product cards.
- **Color Palette:** Purple, white, and light grey for a modern appearance.
- **Typography:** Clear and readable fonts for better usability.
- **Product Cards:** Product images, names, prices, and ratings.
- **Reusable Components:** Consistent buttons, search bars, icons, and cards.
- **Visual Hierarchy:** Important information and action buttons are easy to identify.
- **Mobile-First Design:** Designed for a smooth mobile shopping experience.
- **Interactive Prototype:** Connected screens demonstrate the shopping flow.

## Features
- **Home Screen:** Displays featured products, offers, and popular categories.
- **Search Screen:** Helps users find products quickly.
- **Categories Screen:** Organizes products into categories such as Fashion, Electronics, and Accessories.
- **Product Details Screen:** Shows product images, descriptions, prices, ratings, and available options.
- **Shopping Cart:** Allows users to review selected products and quantities.
- **Checkout Screen:** Displays delivery address, order summary, and payment options.
- **Order Confirmation:** Shows the order success message and order details.
- **User Profile:** Provides access to profile details, saved addresses, and order history.
- **Easy Navigation:** Provides quick access to Home, Categories, Cart, and Profile.
